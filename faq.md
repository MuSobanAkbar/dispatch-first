# Dispatch Repair Shop — Official FAQ & Policies

## Opening Hours & Location (hours)
- **Monday – Friday:** 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- **Saturday:** 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
- **Sunday & Statutory Holidays:** Closed
- **Address:** 123 Main Street, Downtown (Free customer parking is available behind the building).

## Standard Pricing & Quotes (pricing)
- **Diagnostics / Water Damage Check:** $35 flat fee (waived if you proceed with the repair).
- **Phone Screen Replacement:** Starts at $89 for standard models, up to $199 for Pro/OLED models.
- **Phone Battery Replacement:** $59 flat rate (includes part and labor).
- **Laptop / Game Console HDMI or Charging Port:** $110 – $150 depending on model.
- **PC Tune-Up, Dust Cleaning & Thermal Paste:** $65.
- **Exact Quotes:** If a device isn't listed here, customers can bring it in for a quick estimate.

## Booking an Appointment (booking)
- Customers can book a repair slot by replying with their preferred day, time, and device model.
- Walk-ins are welcome, but booked appointments get priority same-day turnaround.
- Most phone screen and battery repairs take 45–60 minutes when booked in advance.

## Repair Status & Turnaround Times (status)
- Standard phone repairs are completed same-day.
- Laptop, console, and micro-soldering repairs typically take 2–3 business days.
- To check on an existing repair, customers must reply with their **Ticket Number** (e.g., #402) or the phone number used at drop-off.

## Other Questions (other)
- We do not buy broken devices for cash, but we offer free electronics recycling at our front counter.
- For job applications or vendor inquiries, email management@dispatchrepair.example.com.
