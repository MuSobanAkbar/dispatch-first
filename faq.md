# Dispatch Bike Shop — Official FAQ & Policies

## Opening Hours & Location (hours)
- **Monday – Friday:** 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- **Saturday:** 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
- **Sunday & Statutory Holidays:** Closed
- **Address:** 123 Main Street, Downtown (Bike racks out front, free parking behind the shop).

## Standard Pricing & Quotes (pricing)
- **Flat Tire / Tube Replacement:** $25 (includes inner tube and labor).
- **Standard Bike Tune-Up:** $75 (brake & derailleur adjustment, chain lube, safety check).
- **Full Overhaul Service:** $160 (deep drivetrain clean, wheel truing, all cables replaced).
- **Brake Bleed (Hydraulic Disc Brakes):** $40 per wheel.
- **Wheel Truing:** $30 per wheel.
- **Exact Quotes:** For suspension service or custom builds, bring your bike in for a free estimate.

## Booking an Appointment (booking)
- Customers can book a service slot by replying with their preferred day, time, and bike type (road, mountain, commuter, e-bike).
- Walk-ins for flat tires are welcome, but booked appointments get priority same-day turnaround.

## Repair Status & Turnaround Times (status)
- Flat fixes and minor adjustments are usually done same-day.
- Full tune-ups and overhauls typically take 2–3 business days.
- To check on a bike currently in the shop, please reply with your **Ticket Number** (e.g., #402) or the phone number used at drop-off.

## Other Questions (other)
- We do not buy used bikes for cash, but we accept old bike donations for our community recycle program.
- For job applications or wholesale parts inquiries, email management@dispatchbikes.example.com.
